Computer Vision Architecture & Reliability Safeguards

Understanding how the genuine MobileNetV2 transfer learning pipeline functions.

1

Pretrained MobileNetV2

Uses 53 inverted residual bottleneck layers pretrained on ImageNet-1K to extract spatial petal, color, and floral texture embeddings.

2

Botanical Fine-Tuning

Unfreezes top convolutional blocks (layers 16+) with differential learning rates to adapt high-level feature maps to real blooming flowers.

3

Rejection Threshold

Enforces a 40.0% confidence cutoff. Out-of-distribution inputs are rejected instead of being arbitrarily assigned to an unrelated class.

4

Verified Taxonomy

Botanical families (e.g. Malvaceae for Hibiscus, Rosaceae for Rose) are retrieved from local verified metadata, not hallucinated.
